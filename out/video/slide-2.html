Three incidents. Same logs. Three different responses.

A human reachable by phone, a human who has not opened the logs in a week, and an agent nobody is watching. In all three cases the text was written by the AI, so text detectors and assistant fingerprinting cannot separate them.
  • Contact — a person is steering, and can be reached
  • Escalate — a person is nominally in the loop, and is not looking
  • Contain — nobody is in the loop at all
The sprint brief proposes three ways out: causal timing, identity probes, vendor attestation. This is about the first.
