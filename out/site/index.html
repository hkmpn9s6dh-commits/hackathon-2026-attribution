What a disguise costs

Waiting costs the attacker 60 seconds per action. Auto-approve costs the defender nothing and erases the channel.

Four things a responder should do

  1. Report the mixture, not the label. "38% of actions waited for a human, median 41 s, n = 214" is auditable. "A supervised human did this" is not.
  2. Hold the other axis fixed. Before believing an intent number, re-run it on actors with the same leash. If it collapses, it was supervision wearing intent's clothes.
  3. Audit a frozen model every month. Ship the matched-pair test with the model; fail closed when it stops separating the classes it was trained on.
  4. Prefer the channel the adversary cannot choose. Attestation binds a claim to a party. Behaviour tells you where to look, not who to call.
